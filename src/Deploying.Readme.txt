AI Interview runtime completion idempotency note:
The AI Interview plugin's final-submit scoring and completion publication are serialized by process-local keyed locks. The AI Interview runtime is supported only on one active application instance. Multi-instance, failover-active, rolling-overlap, and concurrent blue/green runtime deployments are unsupported for exactly-once scoring/publication until a database-backed completion claim or transactional outbox exists. Enabling platform load balancers, proxy configuration, or distributed cache does not make the AI Interview completion lock distributed.
